Trading Guidelines

<1R (3 orders)

1R - 2R (1 order)
Set 1/3 TP at 2R RTH, set OCO sell stop cut loss full position at breakeven (ORCL 10 units & 30 units)
>2R (1+1 order)
Wake up out TP/CL 2/3 below 21ema low band at close, set sell stop cut loss full position at breakeven or 21ema low band, whichever higher. (ORCL 20 units)
